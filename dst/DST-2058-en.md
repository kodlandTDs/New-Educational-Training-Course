## Section 2 — Knowledge check (12 questions, 1 point each)

### Q1. Lesson 1. FlipAnim will not load for two of your students. What is the planned alternative?

- A. **Use BrushNinja** ✅
- B. Skip the animation and start Canva early
- C. Have them watch a classmate and copy the frames later
- D. Have them draw the frames on paper and photograph them

> *Feedback:* BrushNinja is the designated backup editor. Like FlipAnim it works without registration, so no personal data is requested.

### Q2. Lesson 1. A student's animation runs correctly but the letters look rough, and there are eight minutes left. What do you prioritise?

- A. Redrawing the letters so the result looks polished
- B. Adding a background so the GIF looks finished
- C. **That the animation works — legible and functional comes before pretty** ✅
- D. Starting the Canva registration early to save time next class

> *Feedback:* The objective of Lesson 1 is a working, legible GIF. Prioritise that the animation functions over how the design looks; polish is not the learning goal here.

### Q3. Lesson 1. What is the homework, and what must you never ask for?

- A. Finish the GIF at home; never ask for the file
- B. **Prepare an own or an adult's e-mail for the Canva registration; never ask for the password** ✅
- C. Create a Kodland Sparks account; never ask for the nickname
- D. Watch a Canva tutorial; never ask for a screenshot

> *Feedback:* Registration is the classic Lesson 2 blocker, so remind them a few days ahead. The password is never shared with the tutor.

### Q4. Lesson 2. What should you have prepared before the class starts?

- A. A finished example video for every student to copy
- B. The GIFs from Lesson 1 downloaded onto your own computer
- C. A Canva Pro licence for the group
- D. **Backup e-mail accounts, in case registration fails** ✅

> *Feedback:* Registration reliably creates technical difficulties. Have backup e-mail accounts ready so nobody is stranded at the very first step.

### Q5. Lesson 2. In what format should the video project be created?

- A. Horizontal 16:9
- B. Square 1:1
- C. **Vertical** ✅
- D. Whatever the student prefers

> *Feedback:* Vertical format. It is what the final video-presentation is built around across all four lessons.

### Q6. Lesson 2. What must be configured before the submission activity?

- A. **Manual review, with a maximum score greater than zero** ✅
- B. Automatic review with a maximum score of zero
- C. Nothing — submissions are checked automatically
- D. A shared Canva folder for the group

> *Feedback:* Enable manual review with a maximum score greater than zero. Otherwise the submission cannot be graded. The share flow is Share → Anyone with the link → View → Copy link.

### Q7. Lesson 3. A student did not bring a photo for their interests scene. What do you have ready?

- A. A photo of yourself they can use
- B. Nothing — they skip the scene and catch up at home
- C. A stock photo from Canva Pro
- D. **A neutral backup avatar** ✅

> *Feedback:* Always have a neutral backup avatar. It keeps the student moving and removes any pressure to share a personal photo.

### Q8. Lesson 3. Why should you not let students start many AI generations one after another?

- A. The results get worse each time
- B. **Free AI credits are limited** ✅
- C. It slows down the whole classroom's connection
- D. The AI stops removing backgrounds correctly

> *Feedback:* Free credits are limited. Also check the image contains no personal data and stays inside the frame.

### Q9. Lesson 3. A student's scene has a layer problem and they ask you to fix it. What is the right move?

- A. Take the mouse and fix the layer order, then explain what you did
- B. **Give feedback as specific strength → question to improve → support, and let them fix it themselves** ✅
- C. Tell them to undo everything and start the scene again
- D. Ask the AI to regenerate the image with the correct layers

> *Feedback:* Show the technical flow once, then let the student experiment. Do not correct the project in place of the student — that is where the ownership is lost.

### Q10. Lesson 4. Ten minutes are left and no one has exported yet. What is the sanctioned simplification?

- A. Skip publishing to Kodland Sparks
- B. Export without audio
- C. Publish only the first scene
- D. **Music only plus one sound effect** ✅

> *Feedback:* The simplified version is music plus one sound effect. Publishing to Sparks is the point of the lesson — never drop that.

### Q11. Lesson 4. Which of these will break the export?

- A. **Closing Canva before the processing finishes** ✅
- B. Adding more than one audio track
- C. Using a vertical format
- D. Exporting before adding the third scene

> *Feedback:* Do not close Canva before the export finishes processing. Afterwards, verify every page downloaded and that the video actually has audio.

### Q12. Lesson 4. How is permission to use a student's work in Kodland advertising handled?

- A. It is automatic once the project is public on Sparks
- B. The tutor decides on the student's behalf
- C. **It must always be voluntary, never forced** ✅
- D. It is requested by e-mail once the course ends

> *Feedback:* Always voluntary. A student saying no must carry no consequence for their grade or their feedback.
